Job Radar Flows

Every path through the app: how a search is created, how a scan finds and checks jobs, how matches are scored, and when an email goes out. Use it to train new users or to find where something went wrong.

02

Create a search (chat)

The chat builds a search profile. It reads what you type or attach, fills in what it can, and asks only for what's missing.

flowchart TD
  IN[Your first message] --> T[Text description]
  IN --> AT[Attach: PDF, Word, text]
  IN --> SS[Screenshot of a job post]
  IN --> VO[Voice: speech typed into the box]
  AT --> RD[Read the file]
  SS --> IMG{Anthropic key set?}
  IMG -->|Yes| RD
  IMG -->|No| NOTE[Image saved; asks you to type key details]
  VO --> RD
  T --> UND[Understand: titles, skills, years, cities,
remote/hybrid, salary, industries]
  RD --> UND
  UND --> Q[Ask the next missing question]
  Q --> ANS[Your answer]
  ANS --> SIDE{Does it mention
something else too?}
  SIDE -->|City, years, salary,
arrangement, email| SAVE[Save that field too
reply: Got it, ...]
  SIDE -->|No| FILL[Fill the asked field]
  SAVE --> AGAIN{Was the asked
question answered?}
  AGAIN -->|No| Q
  AGAIN -->|Yes| NEXT
  FILL --> NEXT{More missing?}
  NEXT -->|Yes| Q
  NEXT -->|No| CARD[Summary card]
  CARD --> EDIT[Type a change:
add Pune, remove seo skill, make it hybrid]
  EDIT --> CARD
  CARD --> SV[Save / Save and scan now]

Question order

  1. Job titles (confirm the ones found)
  2. Years of experience
  3. Locations
  4. Remote jobs at international companies?
  5. Work arrangement: Remote, Hybrid, Onsite, Any
  6. Must-have skills (confirm)
  7. Minimum salary (optional)
  8. Anything to avoid (optional)
  9. Email for the daily report (optional)
  10. Name for the search

Questions already answered by your description are skipped. A resume never decides the work arrangement or salary; those are always asked.

How it reads your answers

  • add, also, include add to a list; remove, drop take away; anything else replaces.
  • City names are spotted anywhere, including misspellings and short forms: bengular, banglore, hyd, vizag, chenai, bombay, gurgoan.
  • A plain number for salary means lakhs per year: 7 becomes 7 LPA.
  • Text that doesn't look like a job title is never saved as one; the chat says so.
  • "add city Kakinada" adds a city it doesn't know.
  • "also add location" with no city makes it ask which cities.
You: add bengular  →  Job Radar: Got it. I read 'bengular' as Bengaluru. Locations now: Visakhapatnam, Bengaluru.
03

Scan

One scan runs every active search. While it runs, a radar animation shows, the log fills in, and the Scan button becomes a red Stop scan button.

flowchart TD
  ST[Scan now or scheduled time] --> LK{Another scan running?}
  LK -->|Yes| BUSY[Shows: already running]
  LK -->|No| NET{Job sites reachable?}
  NET -->|No| FAIL[Status Failed:
no internet or blocked]
  NET -->|Yes| EB[Read employer job boards
Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby,
SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee]
  EB --> JB[Read job boards
Remotive, Remote OK, Jobicy,
We Work Remotely, Adzuna if on]
  JB --> STO[Store jobs
new, changed, reposted]
  STO --> CL[Jobs gone from an employer board
are marked Closed]
  CL --> DD[Remove duplicates across sources
prefer the employer's own link]
  DD --> PER[For each saved search]
  PER --> MT[Match and score]
  MT --> VF[Check top 30 links that
aren't from employer boards]
  VF --> FR[Sort by freshness and
new vs already sent]
  FR --> RP[Build report, save HTML]
  RP --> EM{Email set up and
search email on?}
  EM -->|Yes| SEND[Send email]
  EM -->|No| KEEP[Results in the app only]
  SEND --> REC[Remember what was sent]
  KEEP --> REC
  REC --> DONE[Status Completed or
Partially completed]
  EB -. Stop scan .-> STOP[Status Scan stopped
nothing emailed]
  JB -. Stop scan .-> STOP
  MT -. Stop scan .-> STOP
  • If one source fails, the scan carries on and lists the failure under "Source failures" in the report. The status is then Partially completed.
  • Employer boards that don't exist are skipped and checked again after 14 days.
  • Stop finishes the current step, then stops. Jobs read so far are kept; nothing is emailed.
  • If email sending failed, those jobs are not marked as sent, so they go out in the next email that works.
04

Matching and ranking

flowchart LR
  J[Job] --> T{Title related to
your job titles?}
  T -->|No| X[Skip]
  T -->|Yes| H{Hard rules}
  H -->|Fails| CM{Only the city
or remote area?}
  CM -->|Yes| CLOSE[Close match]
  CM -->|No| X
  H -->|Passes| SC[Score 0 to 100]
  SC --> TH{Score 55 or more?}
  TH -->|Yes| FULL[Full match]
  TH -->|No| CLOSE

Hard rules (any one fails the job)

  • On your avoid list
  • Internship or trainee role, unless you want one
  • Principal, staff, director or head roles when your experience is under 8 years
  • Onsite or hybrid outside your cities
  • Remote but limited to another country, e.g. "Remote (USA only)"
  • International remote when you chose India only
  • Asks for more than 2 years above your experience
  • Stated salary below your minimum (an unstated salary never fails)

Score weights

PartWeightHow it's measured
Skills30%Your must-have skills found in the job (3–4 found counts as full)
Experience25%Years asked vs your years
Responsibilities20%Title and description vs your job titles
Location10%City or remote area fit
Preferences10%Industry, work arrangement
Pay5%Meets your minimum, when stated

A skill the job doesn't mention is listed as "Not mentioned in the job". A skill the job wants that isn't in your search is "not established in your profile". Neither is treated as a confirmed gap.

Results and email report

flowchart TD
  M[Full matches] --> N{Sent before?}
  N -->|No| PUB{Posted when?}
  N -->|Yes, unchanged| OPEN[Reported earlier and still open]
  N -->|Yes, changed or reposted| CH[Previously reported jobs that changed]
  PUB -->|24 hours| S1[Top opportunities, last 24 hours]
  PUB -->|7 days| S2[Other strong matches, last 7 days]
  PUB -->|Unknown| S3[Unknown publication date]
  PUB -->|Older| S4[Older but still open]
  FEW{Fewer than 5 new
full matches?} -->|Yes| CL[Close matches, up to 10,
each says what it misses]

Every job card shows

  • Title, company, match score
  • Location, work arrangement, job type
  • Posted date (or "unknown"), first seen, last verified
  • Salary and experience asked, when stated
  • Why it matches, what's missing or uncertain
  • Job description (short version)
  • Apply button, company careers page, source link

Email subject

  • Job Radar: 3 new matches – Vinay
  • Job Radar: 1 new match + 6 close – Vinay
  • Job Radar: 8 close matches to review – Vinay
  • Job Radar: no new matches today – Vinay

Each email also lists source failures and pre-filled searches for LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Foundit, Glassdoor, Wellfound and Dice, which aren't scanned automatically.

10

Updates, data, uninstall

Updates

When new program files arrive, a yellow bar says "Job Radar was updated". Restart now starts the new version and reloads the page. Searches and settings are kept.

Where data lives

  • data\jobradar.db: searches, jobs, history, what was sent
  • data\attachments: resumes, screenshots, voice notes
  • reports\: one HTML report per search per scan
  • config\settings.json: email and keys, on this PC only

Uninstall from Start menu → Job Radar → Uninstall, or Windows Apps and features. It removes the scheduled task and shortcuts and asks whether to delete your searches and reports too.

11

Statuses and labels

Job status

  • Verified active listed in the employer's live job feed
  • Appears active application page loads and shows the job
  • Unable to verify blocked, redirected or not checked
  • Closed / expired gone from the feed or page says closed

Freshness

  • Posted < 24 h, < 3 days, < 7 days, older but still open
  • Post date unknown: the source gives no date; "first seen" is when Job Radar found it, never shown as the post date

Scan status

  • Scanning running now
  • Completed every source answered
  • Partially completed some sources failed; listed in the report
  • Scan stopped you pressed Stop; nothing emailed
  • Failed no internet, or the scan crashed

Change labels

  • Updated since last report: the job text changed
  • Reposted / reopened: closed before, or its post date changed
